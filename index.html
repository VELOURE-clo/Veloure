Good to Know

Frequently Asked

What sizes do you offer?

Every Drop 01 piece runs XS–XXL. Fits are cut boxy and relaxed by design — if you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up. Full measurements are in the Size & Fit guide above.

What are the pieces made from?

The Signature Rib Tee is 300 GSM heavy rib jersey, 97% cotton / 3% elastane. The Longsleeve is 100% compact cotton with a mercerized finish, 240–250 GSM. The Relaxed Wide-Leg Jeans are 100% cotton raw indigo denim, 14.5oz, 3x1 right-hand twill.

How do I care for VELOURE pieces?

Machine wash cold (30°C), do not bleach, hang dry in shade, iron on low if needed, do not dry clean. The jeans are raw / unwashed denim and will fade naturally with wear.
